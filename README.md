## Before You Arrive: Quick Checklist

1. **Check your entry requirements.** Confirm the rules for your passport, travel dates and route with the immigration authority and your airline. Read the [China Visa and Entry Guide](https://china-revealed.com/articles/china-visa-entry-guide-for-foreigners/) ⭐ and [240-Hour Visa-Free Transit](https://china-revealed.com/articles/china-240-hour-visa-free-transit/).
2. **Set up mobile payment.** Link a foreign card to Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly: [How to Pay in China](https://china-revealed.com/articles/how-to-pay-in-china/) ⭐.
3. **Sort out internet.** Buy an eSIM or roaming plan and test it: [China SIM vs eSIM](https://china-revealed.com/articles/china-sim-vs-esim/).
4. **Install the core apps.** Alipay, WeChat, Amap, DiDi, Trip.com and a translation app: see [Essential Apps](#essential-apps) below.
5. **Plan your first day.** Airport transfer, hotel check-in and addresses in Chinese: [Your First Day in China](https://china-revealed.com/articles/first-day-in-china/).
